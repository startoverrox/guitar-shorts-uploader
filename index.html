guitar-shorts uploader

guitar-shorts uploader is a personal tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and uploads short guitar performance videos, made by the owner, to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video to the owner's YouTube channel as a private video with a scheduled publish time.
  • Sets the video's title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Reads view counts of the owner's own videos to choose better titles and thumbnails for later uploads.

Who can use it

Only the owner. It is not offered to other people and has no sign-up.
