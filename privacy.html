Privacy Policy — guitar-shorts uploader

Effective date: 2026-11-15

guitar-shorts uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own guitar videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel, and to set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and publish time.
  • View statistics (such as view counts) of videos on that same channel.

How the data is used

The upload permission is used only to upload videos the owner made. View statistics are used only to choose titles and thumbnails for the owner's later uploads. The app does not read, change or delete anything else in the Google account.

Storage

The access token Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and keeps no copy anywhere else.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account page "Third-party apps & services" (myaccount.google.com/connections). Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also ends the app's access.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can be filed as an issue at github.com/startoverrox/guitar-shorts-uploader/issues.
